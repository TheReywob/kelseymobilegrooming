Services & pricing

Every dog is different. Here’s where pricing starts and what you can add to make the visit work for your pet.

Basic bath & brush

Starting at $85

The base charge covers bringing the mobile salon to your home and a basic groom for the smallest clients.

Prices vary by size, breed-specific haircut, coat type and condition, requested services and behavior. An estimate is provided when booking; the final price is determined after an in-person evaluation.

Haircuts: currently available for dogs up to 45 lb. Larger dogs may be considered for bath and brush-outs.
