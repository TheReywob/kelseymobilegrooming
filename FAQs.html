Good to know before you book

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about the mobile grooming experience and what to expect.

What’s different about mobile grooming?

The grooming salon comes to your home, so there’s no trip to drop off or pick up your pet. Kelsey works with one client at a time and completes the groom from start to finish, without time in a salon drying kennel.

What’s included in the starting price?

A basic bath and brush includes a bath, blow dry, brush out, nail trim and ear cleaning. It starts at $85 for the smallest clients. See pricing and add-ons.

Is there a size limit?

Haircuts are currently available for dogs up to 45 lb. Larger dogs may be considered for bath and brush services. Contact Kelsey to discuss your dog.

How is the final price determined?

An estimate is provided when you book. The final price is set after an in-person evaluation and depends on size, breed-specific haircut, coat type and condition, behavior and requested add-ons.

What if my dog is nervous or may bite?

Please share any behavior or handling concerns before the appointment. Kelsey will do her best to work with your dog safely. A surcharge may apply for aggressive behavior. If a groom is started but cannot be completed because of behavior, payment for the groom is still expected.

Can mats be painful?

Yes. Tight mats can pull at the skin and may cause discomfort or irritation. Please contact Kelsey if your dog is becoming tangled so you can discuss a safe next step.

Is there an extra fee if fleas or ticks are found?

Yes. The $8 flea and tick shampoo treatment is added to the groom if fleas or ticks are found on your pet.

Still have a question?

Tell Kelsey a little about your pet and she’ll be happy to help.
